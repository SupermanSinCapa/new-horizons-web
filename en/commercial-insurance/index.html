01For general business

Backing for your company, your team and your vehicles.

  • 01

    General Liability

    Commercial General Liability

    Protects your business if a client or third party is injured at your premises or because of your work, or if you damage someone else’s property. Covers medical costs, damages and legal defense.

  • 02

    Workers’ Compensation

    Employee injuries

    Pays medical costs and part of the wages of employees who are injured or become ill because of their work. In Florida it is required for construction businesses with 1 or more employees and for all other businesses with 4 or more employees.

  • 03

    Workers’ Comp Exemption

    State certificate

    Some business owners and corporate officers can apply to the State of Florida for a certificate exempting them from covering themselves. We help you see if you qualify and set up coverage for your employees if you need it.

  • 04

    Commercial Auto

    Work vehicles

    Insures the cars, vans and pickups your business uses for work. Covers accidents, injuries to others and damage to your vehicles — something a personal auto policy does not cover.

  • 05

    Commercial Umbrella

    Extra liability

    An extra layer of protection that kicks in when a lawsuit or accident exceeds the limits of your other commercial policies.

02Trucking & transport

Protection for your truck, your cargo and your operation.

  • 01

    Primary Liability

    Required by law

    Pays for medical or property damage you cause to other people and their vehicles with your truck. It is required by law, and minimum limits depend on the type of cargo and whether you operate inside or outside Florida.

  • 02

    Physical Damage

    Truck & trailer

    Pays to repair your truck and trailer after a collision or damage from theft, fire, vandalism or severe weather.

  • 03

    Motor Truck Cargo

    Freight

    Protects you if the freight you haul is damaged, lost or ruined during the trip.

  • 04

    Non-Trucking Liability

    Bobtail

    If you are an independent trucker leased to a company, this coverage protects you outside of dispatch — legally and financially — when you drive your truck without a load or off duty.

  • 05

    General Liability for Truckers

    Your premises

    Protects against injuries or property damage that happen at your premises, such as your yard, warehouse or office.
