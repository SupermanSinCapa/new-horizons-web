01Coverages

Protect what you use to live, travel and have fun.

  • 01

    Manufactured & Mobile Homes

    Primary, seasonal or rental

    Your mobile home deserves the same protection as any other home. We help you find a policy that covers the structure, your belongings and your liability, whether it is your primary residence, a seasonal home or a rental.

  • 02

    Motorhomes

    On the road and parked

    Travel with peace of mind knowing your motorhome is protected on the road and when parked. We cover the vehicle, your belongings on board and your liability, whether you use it for weekend getaways or live in it full time.

  • 03

    Motorcycles & Off-Road Vehicles

    Motorcycles · ATVs · UTVs

    For those who love the freedom of two wheels or going off the pavement. We insure motorcycles, ATVs, UTVs and other off-road vehicles, with options to protect your gear, accessories and liability.

  • 04

    Recreational Vehicles (RV)

    Campers · Fifth wheels · Park models

    Campers, fifth wheels, park models and more. We offer coverage options designed for life on the road, so your adventures don’t turn into an unexpected expense.

  • 05

    Travel Trailers

    Towable campers

    Protect your travel trailer on the road, at the campground or stored at home. Coverage for damage, theft, personal belongings and more.

  • 06

    Boats & Watercraft

    Boats · Pontoons · Jet skis · Sailboats

    From fishing boats and pontoons to jet skis and sailboats. We insure your boat, its motor, the trailer and your liability on the water, so all you have to worry about is enjoying it.
