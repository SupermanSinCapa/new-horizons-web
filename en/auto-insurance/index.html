01Coverage levels

Three levels, from basic to full protection.

PIP

What’s included
$10,000 PIP + $10,000 Property Damage Liability (PDL).

Drivers who need to meet Florida’s legal requirement at the lowest cost.

02Liability

What’s included
PIP + PDL + Bodily Injury Liability (BIL).

Drivers who want protection if they cause an accident where other people get hurt.

03Full coverage

Most complete

Full Cover

What’s included
Everything above + Collision + Comprehensive (and optional Uninsured Motorist).
Who it’s for
New, financed or leased cars, or anyone who wants to protect their own vehicle.

02What each coverage means

Understand your policy before you sign.

PIPPersonal Injury Protection
Pays 80% of your medical expenses and part of your lost wages if you are injured in an accident, no matter who was at fault, up to $10,000. Required in Florida.
PDLProperty Damage Liability
Pays for damage you cause to someone else’s property, like their car or a fence. Required, $10,000 minimum.
BILBodily Injury Liability
Pays for injuries you cause to other people in an accident where you are at fault. Not required for most drivers, but highly recommended and mandatory in some cases (SR-22 and FR-44).
CollisionCollision coverage
Pays to repair your car if you crash, regardless of fault.
ComprehensiveComprehensive coverage
Pays for damage to your car from causes other than a crash: theft, vandalism, fire, flood, hail or falling trees.
UMUninsured Motorist
Protects you if you are hit by someone with no insurance or not enough insurance.

Note“Full Cover” is not a legal term. It is the common name for a policy that includes Collision and Comprehensive on top of liability coverage.

03Non-Owner insurance

Drive but don’t own a car?

Non-Owner insurance covers you when you drive a borrowed or rented vehicle. It is also the ideal option if you need to file an SR-22 or FR-44 to get your license back and don’t have a vehicle in your name.

05SR-22 & FR-44 in Tampa

Get your license back. We handle the filing.

If you lost your license due to a violation, the State of Florida may require an SR-22 or FR-44 to reinstate it. They are not insurance policies but certificates your insurer files with the state to prove you carry the required coverage.

SR-22FR-44
What it isA certificate your insurer files with the state to confirm you carry the minimum required coverage.A similar certificate with much higher coverage limits.
When it’s requiredAfter serious violations: driving uninsured, too many points, accidents, a suspended license.After a conviction for driving under the influence of alcohol or drugs (DUI).
Minimum coverage$10,000 per person / $20,000 per accident bodily injury + $10,000 property damage.$100,000 per person / $300,000 per accident bodily injury + $50,000 property damage.
Typical duration3 years3 years
